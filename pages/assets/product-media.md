These images are derived from screenshots of the installed kAIgi 0.5.5 application (build 57). They preserve the application layout while presenting fictional demonstration content. They are not screenshots of a redesigned application.

- `product-capture.webp`: actual empty Capture layout with an illustrated fictional transcript replacement. This image does not establish transcription accuracy or timing.
- `product-library.webp`: actual Document Workbench showing the fictional one-page document *Reading as Thinking* and its Apple translation.
- `product-notes.webp`: actual Notes panel containing a saved fictional reading note beside the same document.
- `product-translation-detail.webp` and `product-notes-detail.webp`: image-editor derivatives isolating the respective existing inspectors for mobile use. Controls and fictional examples follow the same full-window references; no new application action is introduced. Full-window originals remain available through the enlargement links.

No private meeting content or personal document names are included. Only clean reference screenshots containing the fictional sample were submitted to the image editor. Source screenshots are not part of the public asset set.

`kaigi-introduction.mp4` is a 36-second, 1920 × 1080 H.264 / AAC motion film. Japanese narration uses the installed macOS Kyoko Enhanced synthetic voice. The ambient score and editorial transition sounds are an original procedural composition (96 BPM, D major), with music ducked under the voice. No licensed commercial music or private source audio is included.

The film uses kinetic typography, an editorial waveform, progressive reveals of fictional transcript and note text, match cuts into complete paragraph pairs and the existing Notes inspector, and an animated pull-back across the three workflows. Meaningful product footage ends above pixel 780 of the 1080-pixel canvas, leaving a lower caption band. These motions reconstruct a demonstration from the privacy-safe stills: they are not a screen recording of real-time processing and do not establish recognition accuracy, latency, or automatic links between meeting and document content. This provenance record documents the fictional, edited demonstration.
